# Retail & Discount Impact Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to uncover business insights related to sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and discount impact. The analysis focuses on identifying profitable products, loss-making categories, regional performance, and monthly sales trends using data analytics and visualization techniques.

The project combines **Python** for data cleaning and analysis with **Power BI** for interactive dashboard creation.

---

## Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance
* Identify top-performing and loss-making products/categories
* Understand the impact of discounts on profit
* Track monthly sales and profit trends
* Compare regional performance
* Create an interactive business dashboard

---

## Tools & Technologies

* **Python**
* **Pandas**
* **NumPy**
* **Matplotlib**
* **Seaborn**
* **Power BI**

---

## Dataset Features

The dataset includes:

* Order Date
* Region
* Category & Sub-Category
* Sales
* Profit
* Discount
* Quantity
* Customer Information

---

## Key Insights

* Phones and laptops generated the highest sales and profit.
* Products like chairs, tables, and sofas showed losses despite decent sales.
* Sales and profit declined from January to March.
* Higher discounts negatively impacted profitability.
* Best sales and profit were observed around a 10% discount range.
* The West region performed better compared to other regions.

---

## Business Recommendations

* Reduce excessive discounts on low-margin products.
* Focus more on profitable product categories.
* Improve pricing strategy for furniture products.
* Monitor declining monthly trends to optimize sales campaigns.

---

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how data analytics can help businesses improve profitability, optimize discounts, and make data-driven decisions through visual insights and dashboard reporting.
